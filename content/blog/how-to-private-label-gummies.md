Gummies are the fastest-growing format in the supplement industry — and for good reason. They are easy to take, easy to love, and easy to brand. For a founder looking to launch a supplement brand, private-label gummies are the most accessible entry point: proven formulas, low MOQs, and a format customers actually enjoy.

This guide walks through the entire process, from choosing a formula to seeing your first container leave the factory.

## Step 1: Decide What Your Gummy Is For

Before you talk to any manufacturer, get clear on the product itself. The gummy market is crowded, but the people who win are not selling "a multivitamin gummy" — they are selling a specific outcome.

Ask yourself three questions:

- **Who is the customer?** Adults, kids, or a specific demographic?
- **What is the benefit?** Immunity, sleep, energy, beauty, daily nutrition?
- **What differentiates you?** A unique flavor, a clean formula, a specific dosage?

Your answers shape everything downstream — the formula, the shape, the packaging, and the story you tell on the label.

## Step 2: Pick Your Formula

There are two paths to a gummy formula:

**White label (fastest).** Your manufacturer already has mature, market-ready formulas you can launch under your own brand. This is the lowest-risk option: the formula is proven, the MOQ is low, and your lead time is short.

**Custom formulation (more differentiation).** You work with the manufacturer's R&D team to build a bespoke formula around your exact active ingredients and dosages. Slower and higher MOQ, but you own an exclusive product nobody else can copy.

A reliable gummy manufacturer should offer both. At MILDY Health, white-label gummies start from as little as **500 bottles**, and custom formulations include full R&D support and IP ownership.

## Step 3: Choose Your Dosage Form Details

Once the formula is set, the details matter:

- **Pectin or gelatin base.** Pectin gummies are vegan and have a softer texture; gelatin gummies are more traditional and slightly chewier.
- **Sugar-free options.** Stevia and erythritol-sweetened gummies are popular for wellness-focused brands.
- **Shape and color.** Custom shapes (bears, letters, branded icons) require tooling but make the product unmistakably yours.
- **Active ingredients.** Multivitamin, omega-3, biotin, vitamin C, melatonin — each requires different handling and stability testing.

These decisions affect cost, shelf life, and how the product is marketed. Your manufacturer's team should guide you through each one based on your target market.

## Step 4: Understand MOQ and Cost

MOQ (minimum order quantity) is the single biggest gatekeeper for new brands. Ask directly:

- What is the MOQ per SKU?
- Does the MOQ include packaging?
- What are the costs for custom tooling, if any?

A typical starting point for white-label gummies is **500–2,000 bottles** per SKU. If a manufacturer quotes you a MOQ in the tens of thousands on your first order, they are not built for new brands.

## Step 5: Design Packaging and Labeling

Your label must do two jobs: sell the product and comply with the law.

Work with your manufacturer on:

- Bottle or pouch format (and whether a tamper seal is included)
- Label artwork and back-of-pack claims
- Compliance text for your target market (FDA-compliant structure/function claims in the US, EU Novel Food rules in Europe)

Professional manufacturers provide label artwork guidelines and compliance templates so you do not start from a blank page.

## Step 6: Get Regulatory Clearance

Regulation is not a reason to fear gummies — it is a checklist.

- **United States:** Dietary supplements are regulated by the FDA. Your product does not need pre-approval to be sold, but your facility must be FDA-registered and you must follow good manufacturing practices (GMP).
- **European Union:** Novel Food authorization may apply to certain ingredients.
- **Other markets:** Each country has its own registration or notification requirements.

A compliant manufacturer holds the certifications you need (GMP, FDA facility registration, BRCGS, NSF) and can supply the documentation for your market. Read our [guide to FDA registration for dietary supplements](/blog/fda-registration-dietary-supplements) for the full picture.

## Step 7: Production, Quality Control, and Shipping

Once your artwork is approved, production begins:

1. **Raw material inspection** — every incoming lot is tested
2. **Production** — mixing, molding, cooling, coating
3. **In-process control** — weight, hardness, and contamination checks
4. **Finished product testing** — full assay of actives, heavy metals, and microbiology
5. **Batch release** — every batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Your manufacturer handles sea-freight export, customs documentation, and container loading. From artwork approval, typical gummy production lead time is **25–35 days**.

## Choosing the Right Gummy Manufacturer

The single most important decision in your entire project is the factory. Look for:

- **GMP certification** and **FDA facility registration**
- A **dedicated R&D lab** that can develop and test your formula
- **Real production scale** — a facility that actually makes gummies at volume, not just on paper
- **Export experience** — someone who has shipped to your country before
- **Low MOQ and trial orders** — so you can validate the market before committing
