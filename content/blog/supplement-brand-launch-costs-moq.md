"Want to start a supplement brand but not sure what it costs?" is the question we hear most often from new founders. The honest answer: you can launch a credible private-label product for a surprisingly modest budget — if you choose the right model. This guide breaks down every cost so you can build a realistic budget.

## The Three Launch Models

Your cost structure depends entirely on which model you choose.

### Model 1: White Label (Fastest, Lowest Risk)

You pick a proven formula from your manufacturer's catalog, apply your brand, and launch.

- **MOQ:** from 500 bottles per SKU
- **Lead time:** 25–35 days
- **Best for:** first-time founders validating a market

### Model 2: Custom Formulation (More Differentiation)

You build a bespoke formula with the manufacturer's R&D team.

- **MOQ:** higher (typically 2,000+ bottles)
- **Lead time:** 2–4 months including R&D and stability testing
- **Best for:** established brands and brands with a unique selling point

### Model 3: Semi-Custom (The Middle Path)

Take a proven formula and adjust the flavor, dosage, or add a signature ingredient.

- **MOQ:** between the two above
- **Best for:** brands that want differentiation without full R&D cost

## Where Your Money Goes

Here is what a supplement launch actually spends money on:

### 1. Product Manufacturing (the bulk)

This is your largest line item and the one that scales with volume.

- **MOQ cost:** a 500-bottle white-label run of gummies can start around **$1,500–$3,000** depending on formula and packaging
- **Per-unit cost:** drops significantly as volume grows — 5,000 bottles costs far less per bottle than 500

### 2. Formula Development (custom only)

Bespoke formulations involve R&D hours, raw material sourcing, pilot runs, and stability testing. Budget **$2,000–$8,000+** depending on complexity. Many manufacturers (including MILDY) waive development fees if you meet their standard order threshold.

### 3. Packaging and Tooling

- **Standard bottles and labels:** low cost, included or modestly priced per unit
- **Custom gummy shapes or molds:** one-time tooling of **$500–$2,000** per shape
- **Structural custom packaging:** the most expensive packaging option

### 4. Regulatory and Compliance

- **FDA facility registration** and compliance documentation are typically handled by your manufacturer (the facility, not the brand, holds the registration)
- **Independent lab testing** of your finished batch: **$300–$800** per test set depending on market requirements
- **EU Novel Food authorization** (if applicable): a longer and more expensive process — plan ahead

### 5. Branding and Marketing (your choice)

Logo, packaging design, website, and ad spend. This can be as small as a few hundred dollars with design tools, or as large as your budget allows. This is the part you control entirely.

## Real-World Budget Scenarios

**Lean validation launch (single SKU, white label):**
- 500–1,000 bottles: $2,000–$4,000
- Simple labeling + basic branding: $500–$2,000
- **Total: roughly $2,500–$6,000**

**Full brand launch (3 SKUs, custom or semi-custom):**
- 3 × 2,000+ bottles: $15,000–$30,000
- R&D and stability: $3,000–$8,000
- Tooling and packaging: $1,000–$4,000
- Testing and compliance: $1,000–$2,000
- **Total: roughly $20,000–$45,000**

## How to Keep Costs Down

1. **Start white label.** Validate demand before you invest in custom R&D.
2. **Consolidate SKUs.** Launch 1–2 products well instead of 5 poorly.
3. **Ask about development-fee waivers.** Volume commitments often unlock free R&D.
4. **Choose a manufacturer with real infrastructure.** A factory with in-house R&D, QC lab, and export experience saves you from paying separate vendors to coordinate.
5. **Watch the MOQ, not just the price.** A low MOQ lets you test the market with real customer feedback instead of guessing.

## FAQ

**What is the cheapest way to start a supplement brand?**
White-label products with a low MOQ. At MILDY, gummies and other formats start from 500 bottles, keeping your first order in the low thousands of dollars.

**Do I need FDA approval to sell supplements in the US?**
Dietary supplements do not require pre-market FDA approval, but they must be manufactured in an FDA-registered facility under GMP. Your manufacturer should hold both. See our [FDA registration guide](/blog/fda-registration-dietary-supplements).

**Can I launch with one SKU?**
Yes — this is the smartest way to start. One product, tested with real customers, before you scale.

**Are there hidden costs I should watch for?**
Ask upfront about: custom tooling, labeling minimums, lab testing fees, and whether the MOQ includes packaging. A transparent manufacturer will answer all four in one document.
